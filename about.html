Our Story

The Studio Behind Every Portrait

A Manchester portrait studio built on one belief — that everyone deserves to see themselves at their most beautiful.

Luxeimperial Studio is a Manchester-based portrait photography studio specialising in beautiful, timeless images of life's most meaningful moments.

Based in the heart of Manchester, we create portraits that celebrate people exactly as they are — glowing, confident and completely themselves. From birthdays and maternity milestones to anniversaries, pre-wedding celebrations and family sessions, our work is about capturing the emotion of a moment and turning it into an heirloom you'll treasure for years.

We know that stepping in front of a camera can feel daunting. That's why every session is fully guided: we direct each pose, expression and angle so you never have to wonder what to do with your hands or how to stand. The result is a relaxed, enjoyable experience — and images where you look and feel your absolute best.

Every portrait we deliver is hand-retouched by our own editors for flawless, consistent quality across your entire gallery. And with a professional makeup artist available to book alongside your session, you can arrive, relax, and simply enjoy being celebrated.

— The Luxeimperial Studio Team, Manchester

How It Works

Your Session, Step by Step

01

Get in touch

Tell us the session you have in mind — birthday, maternity, anniversary, pre-wedding or family — along with your preferred date. We'll reply within 24 hours to confirm availability and share your rate card.
